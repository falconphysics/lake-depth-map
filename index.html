Cutting & assembly tips
  1. Red lines cut, blue lines score. Each SVG is drawn at real size in millimeters — import at 100% scale.
  2. Sheet 1 is the top (the shoreline and frame). Each next sheet opens up the deeper water. The backing sheet closes the bottom.
  3. Loose pieces are islands and shoals. When a sheet's cut frees an island, glue it onto the next sheet down — the blue score guide shows where it goes.
  4. Paint or stain before gluing. The legend colors make a good shallow-to-deep gradient. Seal plywood edges if you paint them.
  5. Line up with pins. Run dowels or rod through the corner holes while gluing, then trim them flush.
  6. Most 1/8" (3 mm) plywood works; 8–12 layers look great at 12" wide.
